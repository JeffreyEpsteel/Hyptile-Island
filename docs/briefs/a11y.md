src/i18n/** (one file exists), test/feature-a11y.mjs. src/ui/MainMenu.js is the universe worker's; src/game/CatchMinigame.js is

   list by grepping input.hit/down/claimKey across src.

   for reel/sprint, camera shake and reduce-motion (Player.reduceMotion), mouse sensitivity and invert Y, a fishing assist
   (slower tension change) OFF by default and flagged in the save.
3. i18n: src/i18n/index.js with t( key, fallback, vars ), setLang, onLang, persisted language; en.js and ro.js tables; a
