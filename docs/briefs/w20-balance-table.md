# Wave 20 balance table (earnings per real hour)

Generated by `node test/feature-balance.mjs` from the game's constants (src/features/story/Balance.js has the model and its time
assumptions). A real hour is 1.5 in-game days (DAY_SPEED 0.01 island hours a second). n/a: not open at that stage
(no guns early: no bounties, no navy work, no hijacking). Medians are over the activities open at the stage.

## Findings

- Trade runs do not grow with the hull: the boards' room (routes.roomFor) caps a loop at a few units a good, so the brigantine's 44 crates earn less than the lobster boat's 20 (it sails slower). Bulk lots (master licence, the warehouse) are the late trader's tool; a deeper market for big holds is a trade-side follow-up.
- Wages are cheap: 2 % of the mid-game median, 5 % late (tavern hands $10 a day each). The invariant (< 40 %) holds with room; crew morale, not money, is what a skipper manages.
- Fishing stays a real living: deep water with two crew anglers is the top late activity (the hold, not the bite rate, limits it).
